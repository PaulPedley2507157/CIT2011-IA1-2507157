Personal Development

Reflections on The Richest Man in Babylon by George S. Clason.

The 7 Cures for a Lean Purse

Save at least 10% of everything you earn before paying any bills or expenses. Living below 90% of your income helps to prevent financial stagnation. Investing allows your money to work for you while you sleep. Invests your saved money rather than idly. Only invest in places you trust. cultivate your own powers, to study and become wiser, to become more skilful so that you are able to become more strong minded

Living 90% below my income would save more and also invest more to secure my future and even generations after. Sharpening myself both mentally and physically would allow me to be more confident and prepare me for opportunities that may come my way.

The 5 Laws of Gold

Saving a 10% of your pay creates a growing pool of capital that attracts better financial opportunities over time. It also states that you should make your money work for you and steadily increase by itself. We should ensure to invest only in familiar industries or we would almost be sure to lose. When dealing with money we should not become greedy and fall for unrealistic overnight riches from scammers.

From this text I understand that money is a tool that we should not only work for but allow it to work for us. This is something my father taught me over the years as well. Saving money is also very important as this allows us to rise to opportunities whenever they appear. And finally making smarter investments comes from investing in familiar places.

My Biggest Takeaway

My biggest takeaway from The Richest Man in Babylon is that wealth is built through discipline. By simply investing atleast one tenth of your salary and living well below your salary you can set yourself for a comfortable future. Learning new skills, becoming wiser and stronger both mentally and physically prepares me for opportunities that may arrise in the future.
